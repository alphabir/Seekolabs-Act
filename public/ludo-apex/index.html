Game Modes

Two ways to play,
both of them offline

No lobby to wait for, no account to make, no connection to lose halfway through a match. Open the app and the game is already there.

2 – 4 Players

Pass & Play

Grab a friend, or three. Two to four players share a single device and take turns exactly as they would around a real board. The best version of Ludo is still the one played by people in the same room.

  • Hot-seat turns with a clear active-player marker
  • Left- and right-handed board layouts
  • Resume a match exactly where it stopped
Three Tiers

Versus Computer

Play against AI opponents built from real Ludo strategy, not a random-move stub. Easy is a gentle warm-up. Medium plays a solid blocking game. Hard punishes every loose move you make.

  • Opponents that guard, block and hunt captures
  • One, two or three computer players
  • The same engine drives every player, so it is never rigged
Why Ludo Apex

Built as a board game,
not as an ad slot

Most free Ludo apps are a delivery mechanism for interstitials and coin bundles. This one is a game. Every decision below follows from that.

Offline First

Plays anywhere

No Wi-Fi, no mobile data, no sign-up. Built for flights, commutes, basements and every other place a connected game quietly stops working.

Zero Telemetry

Respects your device

No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDK, no background activity, and nothing about you leaves the phone. There is no account, so there is nothing to leak.

Fair Rules

Competitive, not lucky

A faithful, well-tested rule set with a deterministic engine underneath. Dice are rolled the same way for every player, human or AI, and the code path is identical for both.

Dark & Gold

Designed, not assembled

A dark theme with gold bezels, hand-tuned token art, fluid hops and captures, and a light theme for daytime. Every screen was drawn for this game rather than bought from a template pack.

How It Plays

Five minutes to learn,
a long time to master

The Ludo you already know, implemented without shortcuts.

  1. Roll a six to bring a token out of your yard and onto the track.
  2. Move your tokens around the board, racing all four toward the centre.
  3. Land on an opponent to capture it. Their token goes all the way back home.
  4. Sixes, captures and finishes earn another roll, so a good turn keeps going.
  5. Safe squares protect a token from capture. Learn where they are.
  6. The first player to get all four tokens home wins the match.
